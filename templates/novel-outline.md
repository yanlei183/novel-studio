# Naming Guide

Use consistent naming so projects remain easy to search and maintain.

## Project naming

Prefer:

- `fantasy-epic`
- `cyberpunk-mystery`
- `romance-series`
- `post-apocalyptic-short`

Avoid:

- `my-book`
- `novel-v2`
- `test-project`

## Character naming

Format:

- `character-01-<name>.md`
- `character-02-<name>.md`

Example:

- `character-01-lin.md`
- `character-02-mira.md`

## World naming

Format:

- `world-01-<topic>.md`
- `world-02-<location>.md`

Example:

- `world-01-city-of-glass.md`
- `world-02-salt-mountains.md`

## Chapter naming

Format:

- `01-opening.md`
- `02-escape.md`
- `03-signal-in-the-rain.md`

## Scene naming

Format:

- `scene-01-market.md`
- `scene-02-warehouse.md`

## General principle

Use naming that makes the purpose immediately obvious to a future reader, even without opening the file.
